This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at fahieamusements.com (the "Site"). 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the site.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide a streamlined, responsive, and relevant experience for our community and clients.

### Information You Provide to Us
When you interact with our Site, particularly through our contact form, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide. This may include, but is not limited to:
*   **Identifying Information:** Your first and last name.
*   **Contact Information:** Your email address and phone number (if provided).
*   **Inquiry Details:** Any messages, event dates, locations, or other details you choose to share in the text field of our contact form.

### Information Collected Automatically
When you visit and navigate our Site, our hosting provider automatically collects standard server diagnostic and logging data. This information does not specifically identify you by name but may include:
*   Your IP (Internet Protocol) address.
*   Browser type and version.
*   Operating system.
*   Time and date of your visit.
*   Pages viewed and the referring website address.

We use the information we collect strictly for legitimate business and communication purposes. Specifically, we use your information to:
*   Respond directly to your event inquiries, questions, or feedback submitted through our contact form.

*   Communicate with you regarding our services, provided you have requested such communication.

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.**

To operate our Site efficiently, we rely on trusted third-party service providers. These providers only have access to your information to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are legally or contractually obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.
